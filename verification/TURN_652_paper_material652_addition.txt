
Material composition, finite closure and sealed authority correspondence

The constituent record retains both chemical identity and exact composition. Write c_i=(I_i,q_i), where I_i holds constitution, isotope and required chemical context, and q_i is the exact composition record supplied by the chemical dependency. A material carrier is
    M=([c_1,...,c_n],B,d,O), n>0,
with complete constituent support, bonding records B, declared boundary d and observation/reference records O. No condition q_i=q_j is imposed across different constituents. The source-bound conversion from a Substance constituent preserves its identity and composition separately. Equality of two constituent records is equivalent to equality of both I and q. Equality of carriers requires equality of the four retained coordinates. Thus any relation preserving those coordinates implies the same identity, while the coordinates themselves suffice without another identity rule.

Appending a constituent preserves all earlier pairs (I_i,q_i); appending a bonding record preserves the full earlier bonding sequence; appending an observation preserves its earlier reference pairs. For each extension E_x, removing its last appended coordinate gives P_x(E_x(M))=M. Reversing a finite extension history gives P_h(E_h(M))=M by induction. Every earlier constituent, composition, bonding relation and observation consequently remains available, the declared boundary remains fixed, and nonempty support remains nonempty. A supposed equality E_h(M)=E_h(N) would imply M=N after applying P_h, so extension cannot erase an earlier distinction.

Validation checks the payload digest, the two false source-selection/content-opened flags, both counts, the complete prediction-set digest and the three original inventory/structural-count/derivation file hashes. Every required fragment f must occur in C(b) after case folding. Its receipt retains (source identifier, registered snapshot hash, f, H(source identifier,casefold(f))). The extraction digest is the hash of the complete ordered receipt list; no required fragment is omitted. The first four prediction coordinates describe the material content relation; the last four record the separately tested method conditions, exactly as in the source.

HOW TO CONTRIBUTE A REVIEW
Open a structured GitHub review using the existing template:
https://github.com/MettaMazza/SFT-v4-LEAN-VERIFIED/issues/new?template=independent-review.md
Use only the sections relevant to your finding; questions and short reports are welcome. GitHub sign-in is required to submit. Your submitted issue is public, so omit private correspondence and contact details.

Optional copy-and-fill response form:
https://github.com/MettaMazza/SFT-v4-LEAN-VERIFIED/blob/main/peer-review/FOCUSED_REVIEW_FORM.txt
Choose one question; there is no obligation to assess the whole corpus. You may report an unresolved step or decline further work. Short responses and other formats are welcome.

A focused review of one derivation is welcome. Please identify:
- The paper DOI/version and exact section or statement.
- The original source component and corresponding Lean declaration, where applicable.
- The relevant domain and hypotheses, including those inherited from earlier steps.
- The argument, calculation, reproduction command or counterexample supporting your finding.
- Whether the finding concerns a local step, a translation, an empirical comparison or the stated result, and why.
State what you examined and distinguish it from material you have not examined. A question is also useful when it identifies the exact step that needs explanation.
Post reviews through the applicable PREreview links above, or use the public GitHub review discussion below for any paper. Please disclose relevant affiliations and AI assistance. Independent reviewers retain responsibility for their own assessments.

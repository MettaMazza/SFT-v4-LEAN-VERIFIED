FOLLOW THE EXCLUSION ARGUMENT
An unchanged positive rational ratio p/q would obey p/q = 1 + q/p, hence p² = pq + q². The native proof assumes that equation and constructs q | p². If q != 1, positivity yields q > 1. primeDivisorExists supplies a prime r dividing q; composeDivides and primeProduct then give r | p. commonDivisorDividesGcd contradicts the gcd-one hypothesis, so q = 1.

The proof next uses p | p² and p | pq with dividesSubtract to obtain p | q². After q = 1, divisorBound and positivity force p = 1. Substituting both equalities into the assumed equation yields the final contradiction.

The equation is quantified over all coprime positive integer pairs, not only the six displayed history rows. Review the imported divisibility and prime-support arguments through the linked module's imports when tracing these steps.

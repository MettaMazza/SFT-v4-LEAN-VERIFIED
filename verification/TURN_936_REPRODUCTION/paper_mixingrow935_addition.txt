The complete first quark and lepton alignment rows are already fixed by the shared overlap construction:

Q₁=(8/9,5/9,2/9),    L₁=(5/6,1/2,1/6).

Retaining every channel gives

ΣQ₁=(8+5+2)/9=15/9=5/3,
ΣL₁=(5+3+1)/6=9/6=3/2.

Holding out one complete One returns each row's generating lock:

q=ΣQ₁−1=5/3−3/3=2/3,
ℓ=ΣL₁−1=3/2−2/2=1/2.

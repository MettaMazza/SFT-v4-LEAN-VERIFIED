Two undirected diamond graphs with edges a-b, a-c, b-d and c-d. With stored edge order (a,b), (a,c), (b,d), (c,d), the queue explores b before c and returns [a,b,d], highlighted in teal. Swapping the first two edges makes c enter the queue first and returns [a,c,d]. Both routes have three nodes and two edges. Source order selects a shortest witness.

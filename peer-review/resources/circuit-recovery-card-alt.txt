Five rows start from repeated held labels and change at most two positions to returned. Width 1: R, decoded returned. Width 2: R H, a tie. Width 3: R R H, decoded returned. Width 4: R R H H, a tie. Width 5: R R H H H, held recovered. H means held; R means returned. The first four rows exhibit failure at every smaller positive width. At width five, every mask of at most two changes leaves at least three original labels. A tie has no strict majority. Maria Smith, Ernos Labs; prepared with AI assistance.

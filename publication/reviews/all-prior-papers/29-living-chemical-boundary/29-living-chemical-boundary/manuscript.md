Living organization retains identity through regulated material and energy turnover. Its chemical boundary jointly retains compartment, maintenance, heritable continuation and regulated resource exchange. Smithian Fold Theory gives these conditions an explicit generated preservation grammar: eight binary coordinates, 256 exhaustive alternatives and exactly one preserving form for each of four connected biological obligations. We derive the complete classification and its uniqueness, explain the original positive finite base and lawful-successor closure, and retain the resource, observation and provenance conditions throughout. Autocatalytic closure retains the least complete finite reaction support regenerating every required catalytic role; maintenance retains the transitions restoring identity-defining relations faster than incompatible distinctions accumulate at the declared boundary. The native Lean translation preserves all 1,024 candidate decisions across the four entries. Retained kernel audits identify 475 distinct declarations with empty axiom dependencies. The source comparison preserves seventeen snapshot identities, the 75-claim seal and the complete empirical receipt.

## 1. The biological distinction

A living system is a bounded process network whose identity persists through turnover. The material present at one instant and the organization retained through its transitions play different roles. The original life-organization obligation retains four explicit components: a bounded process carrier; a self-maintaining transition relation; identity through turnover; and a declared viability window. [1–3]

The chemical-boundary statement then requires compartment, maintenance, heritable continuation and regulated resource exchange jointly. Compartment retains the boundary of the process network. Maintenance retains the relations that make its continuing state the same organization. Heritable continuation retains those distinctions through continuation of the organized process. Regulated exchange retains the material and energy transitions by which the bounded network persists. Their joint retention is the original classification condition; the complete chemical reaction carrier remains part of the record.

The four source statements form an ordered chain. Living organization precedes the chemical boundary; that boundary precedes autocatalytic closure; closure precedes maintenance. Every entry also retains the eighteen original foundation, mathematics, information, observation, chemistry and materials dependencies. The first therefore has eighteen registered prerequisites and each successor has nineteen.

Thus the recurrence is stated on the complete network and its required catalytic roles, with its resource inputs still identified.

The comparison retains the original boundary and the original transitions; it introduces no fitted rate or threshold.

Their exact native implementations compare the corresponding source strings. They establish a nonempty carrier inscription, separately reconstructible relation and organization, and a boundary distinct from the relation. These witnesses accompany the full preservation classification below.

## 3. Construction of the complete grammar

For each obligation, extend its scientific tuple to
\[
 c=(C,R,O,B,T,P,G,E).
\]
The last four coordinates retain the complete state-transition-control record \(T\), root-bound forward provenance \(P\), positive finite successor and adverse closure \(G\), and absence of an extra rule \(E\). Each coordinate has exactly the two constructors supplied by the original source: its rejected alternative \(r_i\) and its admitted alternative \(a_i\). The generated carrier is
\[
 \mathcal C=\{r_1,a_1\}\times\cdots\times\{r_8,a_8\}.
\]

The alternatives are content-specific. A rejected carrier erases the biological carrier or retains only an answer. A rejected relation imports, fits or erases the relation. Rejected organization collapses distinctions that the biological question requires. A rejected observation leaves its organism, condition, assay, scale or interval unrecorded. A rejected record omits transitions or controls. Rejected provenance lets authority, targets or a prior answer select the law. Rejected generality retains one favorable specimen. A rejected extension introduces a fit, exception or extra rule.

The admitted first four coordinates are exactly the four tuples in Section 2. The admitted final four retain the complete record, root-bound forcing, the original successor/adverse closure and no extra rule. Consequently no numerical parameter is adjusted to choose a survivor.

The generator visits the Cartesian product in the original coordinate order. With each coordinate having two distinct constructors,
\[
 |\mathcal C|=\prod_{i=1}^{8}2=256.
\]
For completeness, let \(c\) be any value of the generated candidate type. Its first coordinate is one of its two constructors, so it occurs in the outer generator. Its second coordinate occurs in the corresponding next generator. Repeating this argument for all eight coordinates places \(c\) in the resulting list. No other constructor exists at any coordinate. Distinct coordinate tuples differ at a first coordinate and therefore occupy distinct product branches.

The native `candidatesComplete` proof constructs membership through these eight nested product generators for an arbitrary candidate. `generatedCardinality` evaluates the actual list length to 256. The universal quantifier is over the complete generated carrier.

## 4. Exact acceptance and unique survival

Define the source preservation decision by
\[
 A(c)=\bigwedge_{i=1}^{8}(c_i=a_i),\qquad
 a=(a_1,\ldots,a_8).
\]
**Classification theorem.** For every \(c\in\mathcal C\),
\[
 A(c)\iff c=a.
\]
**Proof.** If \(A(c)\), each conjunct fixes one coordinate of \(c\) to its admitted constructor. Equality of all eight fields gives \(c=a\). Conversely, substituting \(a\) makes every conjunct reflexive. Therefore \(A(a)\) holds. If two candidates satisfy \(A\), both equal \(a\), so they equal one another. This proves existence and uniqueness.

The executable source decision uses the exact candidate identifier, formed by joining the eight coordinate names in order. The native `sourceDecisionMatchesCriteria` theorem proves that comparison equivalent to the coordinate conjunction. Its proof first splits the four scientific coordinates into their sixteen combinations and then exhausts the remaining four constructor pairs. `uniqueCriteriaSurvivor` proves the conjunction theorem; `uniqueSurvivor` transfers it to the executable decision. `acceptanceIffExactCriteria` gives the same result as eight explicit constructor equalities.

If any coordinate \(j\) is rejected, \(c_j=r_j\ne a_j\), the corresponding conjunct fails and \(A(c)\) is false. The eight coordinate-rejection theorems implement this elimination. The recorded reason is the first failed coordinate in the original order, so decision provenance remains reconstructible even when several conditions fail.

Applying the theorem to the four obligations yields four unique preserving forms, each drawn from 256 complete alternatives. Thus the combined census contains \(4\cdot256=1,024\) decisions.

## 5. Positive finite extension and retained closure

The original closure construction specifies its base and successor explicitly. At the least positive finite carrier, every required biological distinction and its complete control record are retained. A lawful finite successor preserves existing identities, relations, boundaries and provenance, and appends its new transition and adverse alternatives.

Let \(Q(n)\) denote retention of those distinctions and the complete record after \(n\) generated positive finite stages. The source base gives \(Q(1)\). For the original lawful-successor operation, existing retained fields persist and the new transition and its adverse alternatives are appended. Hence
\[
 Q(n)\Longrightarrow Q(n+1).
\]
Starting at the base, repeated application gives \(Q(n)\) at every positive finite stage generated by that operation. The hypothesis that the successor preserves existing distinctions is explicit in the construction. The appended adverse alternatives remain available for the same preservation decision; an erased boundary or imported relation fails its corresponding coordinate.

The source closure certificate retains the exact result, base, successor, exclusions, three operational witnesses and survivor identity. Native `closureValue` preserves those fields, while `closureData` binds the certificate to the complete decision list. This carries the original depth-independent closure statement and its exact grammar boundary through the executable evidence record.

For autocatalysis, the retained recurrence connects products to required catalytic roles while retaining the complete network and its resources. For maintenance, extension retains both the identity-defining relations and the damage-to-restoration transitions at the stated time and condition boundary. The same closure construction therefore preserves what each obligation actually names.

## 6. Source corpus and exact extraction

\[
 \mathcal T(b)=F(U(W(T(D(b))))).
\]
Here \(D\) decodes UTF-8 with replacement; \(T\) replaces every match of the original tag expression `<[^>]+>` by one space; \(W\) collapses whitespace runs; \(U\) decodes HTML entities; and \(F\) applies Unicode case folding. Whitespace collapse precedes entity decoding. This order preserves the original treatment of entity-generated whitespace and boundary spaces.

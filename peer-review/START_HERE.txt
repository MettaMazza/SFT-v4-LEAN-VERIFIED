CHOOSE A READING ROUTE
These are entry points by subject. Paper numbers refer to the catalogue below; each paper supplies its own derivation, dependencies and source references.

Foundations: begin with paper 6 (least Fold generator), then 36 (generator three, spatial dimensions and boundary rank) and 7 (seven sectors).

Programming languages and formal methods: begin with paper 31 (compiler correctness), then 35 (exact calculator across numerical and interface layers) and 32 (finite Fold computation). Paper 34 covers constructive reachability, cycles and shortest paths.

Quantum information and circuits: begin with paper 30 (information loss and reversibility), then 33 (phase, interference and error correction) and 20 (support uncertainty and Bell-data test).

Chemistry and materials: begin with paper 25 (molecular electronic support), then 27 (configuration graphs and reaction paths), 26 (coordination and chelation) and 28 (isotope accounting). Paper 21 covers crystallography; 22 covers semiconductor junctions; 42 covers chirality.

Particle and nuclear physics: begin with paper 1 (fine-structure constant), 3 (charged-lepton ratios) or 2 (Nickel-62), according to your expertise. Follow the selected paper's stated dependencies.

Gravitation and cosmology: begin with paper 15 (compact-object and wave signatures), then 17 (primordial abundances and cosmic expansion) and 40 (finite quantum gravity).

HOW TO CONTRIBUTE A REVIEW
Optional copy-and-fill response form:
https://github.com/MettaMazza/SFT-v4-LEAN-VERIFIED/blob/main/peer-review/FOCUSED_REVIEW_FORM.txt
Choose one question; there is no obligation to assess the whole corpus. You may report an unresolved step or decline further work. Short responses and other formats are welcome.

A focused review of one derivation is welcome. Please identify:
- The paper DOI/version and exact section or statement.
- The original source component and corresponding Lean declaration, where applicable.
- The relevant domain and hypotheses, including those inherited from earlier steps.
- The argument, calculation, reproduction command or counterexample supporting your finding.
- Whether the finding concerns a local step, a translation, an empirical comparison or the stated result, and why.
State what you examined and distinguish it from material you have not examined. A question is also useful when it identifies the exact step that needs explanation.
Post reviews through the applicable PREreview links above, or use the public GitHub review discussion below for any paper. Please disclose relevant affiliations and AI assistance. Independent reviewers retain responsibility for their own assessments.

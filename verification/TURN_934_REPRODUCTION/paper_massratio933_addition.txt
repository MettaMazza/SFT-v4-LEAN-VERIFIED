
805. The mass-ratio family at every positive depth

The binary count b=2 and generator period c=3 fix the complete combined-ladder support. At each positive integer depth d,

N_d=b c^d=2·3^d.

The light carrier occupies one support cell. Its heavy complement occupies all remaining cells. Their exact fractions and the central carrier are

L_d=1/N_d,    H_d=(N_d−1)/N_d,    C=1/2.

The partition closes because

L_d+H_d=[1+(N_d−1)]/N_d=N_d/N_d=1.

Dividing the complement by the light carrier cancels their shared support:

H_d/L_d=[(N_d−1)/N_d]/[1/N_d]
       =[(N_d−1)N_d]/[N_d·1]
       =N_d−1=2·3^d−1.

This identity holds at every positive depth, rather than being inferred from the displayed initial values. The base depth gives N_1=2·3=6 and H_1/L_1=5. At the successor,

N_(d+1)=2·3^(d+1)=3N_d,
R_(d+1)=3N_d−1=3(R_d+1)−1=3R_d+2.

The first three complete records are therefore

d=1: (N,L,C,H,R)=(6,1/6,1/2,5/6,5),
d=2: (N,L,C,H,R)=(18,1/18,1/2,17/18,17),
d=3: (N,L,C,H,R)=(54,1/54,1/2,53/54,53).

The source operation mass_ratio_family rejects booleans and nonpositive depths, derives N from the existing generated counts and positive power, forms both fractions, divides them and checks the result against the independently counted positive predecessor. It returns all five fields shown above. Depth indexes the family; it is not fitted to a measured mass.

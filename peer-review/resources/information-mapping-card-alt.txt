Diagram of two successive maps on ordered labels aa, ab, ba, bb. The first keeps the first letter: aa and ab map to a; ba and bb map to b. Two of the six original unordered pairs have equal outputs. The second sends both a and b to one, so all six original pairs now have equal outputs. A reverse list retains possible predecessors, not which predecessor supplied a particular run.

THE STORY
In May 2026, Maria Smith began asking what mathematics fundamentally is and why its starting assumptions were needed. Her investigation developed into Smithian Fold Theory through an adversarial back-and-forth with AI. In her account, the One and Fold construction preceded her decision to test it against the fine-structure constant. She is the scientific author; AI assisted the working process and subsequent native Lean translation.

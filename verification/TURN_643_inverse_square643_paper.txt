
Conserved response on the forced rank-two boundary

The boundary-rank derivation retains the two nonnormal directions of the forced three-direction spatial carrier. Complete boundary cells are their ordered pairs. For coordinate supports L and R, pair membership is equivalent to membership of both coordinates, and the number of pairs is |L| |R|. Extending either coordinate retains all earlier pairs and adds its pairs with every member of the other coordinate. At equal generated scale n, the complete count is n². The one-cell base is the single pair, carrying the unchanged source.

For exact positive rational distance ratio r > 1, the existing guarded boundary-growth operation applies repeated positive multiplication at the derived rank two:
    G(r) = r · r.
Its normalized representation satisfies
    numerator(G) denominator(r)² = numerator(r)² denominator(G).
This is the exact rational extension of complete pair-cell growth; no measured exponent is supplied.

The inverse-square operation first validates the source S as an exact positive rational and then applies the original above-One distance guard. It returns the positive quotient
    F(S,r) = S/G(r) = S/(r · r).
Let S = a/b and G = c/d. The quotient's normalized value f obeys
    (a d) denominator(f) = numerator(f) (b c).
Multiplication by the generated boundary growth reconstructs the original source. In cross-product form,
    numerator(f) c b = a denominator(f) d,
which is the preceding quotient identity with its finite products reassociated. Uniqueness of normalized positive ratios therefore establishes
    f · G = S.
The proof applies to every positive source and generated exact positive growth value. If a candidate share x satisfies the same conserved cross-product equation, both x and f represent the same positive ratio (a d)/(b c); uniqueness gives x = f. Conservation and equivalent allocation therefore leave no alternative share.

For a complete finite pair boundary, the existing equal-share construction also retains every cell exactly as an output row. Its conservation equation at equal coordinate count n is
    numerator(S) denominator(share)
      = numerator(share) denominator(S) n².
The complete pair support, both coordinate-successor proofs, no-missing-response theorem and unique conserved allocation connect the rational response to the retained finite boundary construction. The original exact witnesses give F(1,2)=1/4, F(1,3)=1/9 and F(5/7,4/3)·G(4/3)=5/7.

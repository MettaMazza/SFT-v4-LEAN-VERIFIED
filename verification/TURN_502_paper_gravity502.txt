
458. Realization and minimality of the complete geodesic trace

The geometry selection and locally unforced propagation describe the same registered support paths. A supported free path retains each adjacent endpoint pair in the generated curved-support relation. Its complete event list E(p) satisfies

E(empty_a)=[a],
E(p·e)=E(p)++[last(e)],
|E(p)|=N(p)+1.

Induction on the free path establishes that E(p) is a generated geometry trace. Conversely, induction on any generated geometry trace constructs a complete free realization. The retained event begins with the initial word. At an adjacent step from a to b, the construction retains the held source identity, takes the current word w to T(w), appends an empty extra-force record and joins the words exactly. Every generated trace therefore has a free-path realization with precisely its complete event list, unchanged source provenance, empty extra-force list and final word T^N(w_initial).

Let s be the trace returned by the existing shortest-path construction for admitted distinct endpoints. Its free realization p has E(p)=s. For every supported competing free path q, the converse construction gives a generated trace E(q). The previously proved geometry inequality therefore gives

N(p)+1=|s|≤|E(q)|=N(q)+1,

hence N(p)≤N(q). This connects actual selection to minimal free propagation, rather than merely declaring a path minimal. It ranges over every supported competing path and does not fix their resulting words or impose a selected depth cutoff. All original source, recurrence and force records remain attached to p.

The original source boundaries are retained. At an admitted identical endpoint, the geometry result is EmptyOne, while the complete retained-event record has no transition and leaves the initial word unchanged. An endpoint outside the generated carrier is rejected. For distinct admitted endpoints, absence of a selected result is equivalent to the absence of a generated path. The empirical comparison remains the registered GWOSC orbital, propagation and source-trajectory records.

459. Complete inertial-energy source and gravitational boundary closure

A registered gravitational relational change retains its originating inertial-energy event, destination event, complete adjacent propagation trace and both source-curved comparison paths between those endpoints. The source energy is the already derived positive value E=mc² of the originating conversion record. Its preparation identity and complete action record are retained with the transfer; no independent source coefficient enters the construction.

For a complete list of changes C=[c₁,…,c_n], construct the transfer ledger by

L(C)=[(first(c),last(c),c,source_identity(c),action_record(c)) : c∈C].

Projecting the carrier field returns C exactly. Thus no comparison path, propagation trace, energy source or constituent record is discarded in forming the ledger. Every propagation step remains adjacent, every distinct arrival has an adjacent predecessor, and projecting source/action pairs from the complete trace recovers every original source record.

The complete source/destination accounting gives the equality of whole carrier lists

Outgoing(L(C))=Incoming(L(C)).

Applying the source-energy reading to both sides preserves equality of the exact energy-reading lists. The result retains each full carrier and its position in the ledger, rather than checking only the number of transfers.

For a held interior predicate I, an outward transfer belongs to the ledger, begins inside and ends outside. Conversely, every ledger transfer satisfying those two endpoint conditions belongs to the outward record. The existing boundary-conservation derivation then gives

InteriorLossCarriers(I,L(C))=BoundaryOutflowCarriers(I,L(C)).

Applying the exact energy reading gives the corresponding equality of interior-loss and outward-energy lists. Hence an outward field transfer cannot appear without its complete registered source change, and an admitted outward change cannot be omitted from the boundary account. The external boundary retains the original GWOSC source mass-energy, radiated-energy and detector-strain records.

Smithian Fold Theory: A Lean Formalisation of the V3 Model

Maria Smith
Working manuscript — unpublished

Abstract

We present a native Lean formalisation of Smithian Fold Theory V3, preserving its definitions, dependency order, declared domains and zero-axiom, zero-free-parameter constraints. The formalisation begins with the operational root, There Is No Nothing, followed by the structural One and exact positive finite count. At the scope established here, their registered candidate grammars contain two, six and ten classes respectively. Constructive proofs establish exhaustive enumeration, the unique surviving class and acceptance of that survivor for each grammar. The 4,175 audited proofs have no axiom dependencies. The translated classifications agree with the V3 artifacts on every candidate's order, identifier, exact form, decision and reason.

The complete formalisation follows all 2,781 entries in dependency order, preserving their original definitions, derivations, declared constraints, controls and empirical boundaries. Each translated component is bound to its V3 source, and full-model verification requires both native proofs of the registered formal statements and correspondence of the associated executable procedures. Empirical records retain their original evidential status. The results presented here cover 71 completed entries: fifteen Foundation entries, twelve Mathematics entries, twelve Information Science entries and twelve Formal Computation entries and ten Computability entries and ten Computational Complexity entries through Fold Average and Worst Case. SFT distinguishes the structural One, generated positive finite counts and exact positive parts from host implementation indices. Its derivational constraints exclude added axioms, free or fitted scientific parameters, negative proof quantities, irrational or imaginary proof values, floating equality and completed infinity. Formalisation preserves the registered source boundary of each result.

Smithian Fold Theory: A Lean Formalisation of the V3 Model

Maria Smith
Working manuscript — unpublished

Abstract

We present a native Lean formalisation of Smithian Fold Theory V3, preserving its definitions, dependency order, declared domains and zero-axiom, zero-free-parameter constraints. The formalisation begins with the operational root, There Is No Nothing, followed by the structural One and exact positive finite count. At the scope established here, their registered candidate grammars contain two, six and ten classes respectively. Constructive proofs establish exhaustive enumeration, the unique surviving class and acceptance of that survivor for each grammar. The 1,392 audited proofs have no axiom dependencies. The translated classifications agree with the V3 artifacts on every candidate's order, identifier, exact form, decision and reason.
